# Question: How is the timeline dataset collected and maintained?

Hi! We're building a small, open-source gacha game tracker with a similar calendar feature for maintenance and character banners, particularly for the America server. We're currently maintaining the calendar manually, with links to verified announcements, and researching how to make updates more reliable in the future.

Looking through your code, I noticed that [`src/routes/timeline/index.svelte`](https://github.com/MadeBaruna/paimon-moe/blob/main/src/routes/timeline/index.svelte) imports the calendar directly:

```js
import { eventsData } from '../../data/timeline';
```

The entries in [`src/data/timeline.js`](https://github.com/MadeBaruna/paimon-moe/blob/main/src/data/timeline.js) include dates and links to HoYoLAB posts, and the timeline applies server-specific time conversion. Clicking an event leads to the original announcement.

Could you share how you collect and maintain this dataset?

- Is it edited manually from official announcements, generated by scripts, or maintained through a separate tool/repository? If there is a public generator or documentation, could you point us to it?
- Which sources do you use for maintenance windows, character banner start/end times and artwork: HoYoLAB posts, the official website, the in-game announcement feed, or another source? If you use announcement interfaces, how do you locate the relevant announcements and extract their actual event periods rather than their publication/visibility dates?
- How do you distinguish times shared globally, such as maintenance, from times relative to each server? In particular, how do you verify America dates and decide when to use `timezoneDependent`?
- How do you handle corrections, delayed maintenance, missing end times and announcements that contain dates only in images? Is there a manual review step before publishing?

We aren't assuming that the HoYoLAB links imply automatic importing; we're trying to understand the workflow behind the committed timeline data. Any explanation or pointers would be very helpful. Thanks for making the project open source!
